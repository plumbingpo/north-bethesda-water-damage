Signs It Is Time for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Water from a floor above is streaming down interior walls.
  • Floor-level power strips, plugged-in appliances or the breaker panel are now wet.
  • You have no way to locate or get to the shutoff while water continues to pour.
  • Wet carpet, wood flooring or wallboard has stayed saturated longer than a few hours.
  • The water is from a sewer backup or looks dark and smells bad.
  • A water heater tank has failed and is emptying across the floor.
  • Pooled water is creeping over the floor into neighboring rooms or onto the staircase.

How to Prevent Emergency Water Damage Restoration Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Important paperwork and valuables belong above floor level, most of all on the lowest story.
  • Pipes running through unheated areas need insulation to keep them from freezing and bursting.
  • Put leak alarms beneath sinks and beside the dishwasher, washer and water heater.
  • Any trip beyond a few days is a reason to turn the main supply off before you go.
  • Find your main water shutoff today and make sure everyone in the house can close it.
  • Save an emergency restoration company's number in your contacts ahead of time.
